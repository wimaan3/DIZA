## USC Department of Public Safety (University Park Campus)
Emergency, 24 hours: 213-740-4321. Non-emergency, 24 hours: 213-740-6000. Blue light phones on campus connect directly to the DPS 24-hour communications center. Source: dps.usc.edu.

## USC Health Sciences Campus public safety
Emergency: 323-442-1000. Non-emergency: 323-442-1200. Source: dps.usc.edu.

## Safety escorts at USC
Students can request a safety escort through DPS at the non-emergency line, 213-740-6000, or from any blue light phone. Escorts cover the DPS patrol area. If you're being followed or threatened right now, call 213-740-4321 or 911. This line can also take an escort request.

## USC Student Health and counseling, 24/7
USC Student Health has one number for both campuses, 24 hours a day: 213-740-9355 (213-740-WELL). After hours and on weekends, callers can reach a counselor. The same number reaches the 24/7 advocates for relationship and sexual violence support. Source: studenthealth.usc.edu.

## Trojans Alert
Trojans Alert is USC's official emergency text and email notification system. Students are registered automatically. Follow its instructions during a campus emergency. Source: dps.usc.edu.
