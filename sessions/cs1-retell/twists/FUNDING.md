## Pathways, best fit first

### 1. NSF SBIR/STTR, "America's Seed Fund" (NSF 26-510)
- **Funds:** high-risk R&D in nearly any technology area. NSF does not pick topics.
- **Awards:** Phase I up to **$305K** (6-18 mo, I-Corps add-on included). Phase II up to **$1.25M** (about 24 mo).
- **Who applies:** a small business (500 employees or fewer). The PI must be at least 51% employed by the company at award, so student founders must form a company first. A university partner like USC fits the STTR variant.
- **Status:** **Open.** Posted May 2026. Full proposals due **Nov 4, 2026**, **Mar 4, 2027** and **Jul 7, 2027**, then every Nov, Mar and Jul. A full proposal needs an invitation, which you get by submitting a Project Pitch. Each company gets at most 2 pitches per 12 months.
- **DIZA fit:** NSF funds the technical risk, such as multilingual surge triage, flagging vulnerable callers and warm handoff when the network is overloaded, not the Retell integration itself.
- **URL:** https://www.nsf.gov/funding/opportunities/small-business-innovation-research-small-business-technology/nsf26-510/solicitation · https://seedfund.nsf.gov

### 2. NSF I-Corps (Regional, then National Teams, NSF 25-549)
- **Funds:** customer discovery only, at least 100 interviews. R&D and prototyping are not allowed.
- **Awards:** National Teams up to **$50K** over 12 months.
- **Who applies:** a US college or university, here USC. The team is an Entrepreneurial Lead (a student), a Technical Lead/PI and an Industry Mentor. If the PI has no recent NSF award, the team first completes a **Regional I-Corps** course at an NSF I-Corps Hub and gets a recommendation letter.
- **Status:** **Open, proposals accepted anytime.** You must be invited after sending an Executive Summary. The page was updated Oct 6, 2026.
- **DIZA fit:** interviews with LA 311/EOC/911 staff and campus emergency managers check the buyer before any SBIR pitch.
- **URL:** https://www.nsf.gov/funding/opportunities/nsf-national-innovation-corps-teams-nsf-national-i-corps-tm/nsf25-549/solicitation

### 3. FEMA Homeland Security Grant Program, UASI/SHSP (customer-funded)
- **Funds:** state and local planning, equipment, training and exercises.
- **Size:** FY2026 total **$1.064B**, of which UASI is **$584.3M**.
- **Who applies:** the State Administrative Agency (Cal OES). The LA/Long Beach urban area and other localities receive funds as subrecipients. DIZA would be a vendor they buy from.
- **Status:** **Recurring every year.** FY2026 awards were announced **Sep 23, 2026**. Cal OES reports a 20% holdback tied to election-security conditions, and news sources report minimum spending shares for law-enforcement terrorism prevention.
- **DIZA fit:** a city or county could fund DIZA as surge-communications and public-warning capability in its UASI project list.
- **Not verified:** whether DIZA's specific costs are allowable. Check against the NOFO and Cal OES.
- **URL:** https://www.fema.gov/grants/preparedness/homeland-security

### 4. FEMA Emergency Management Performance Grant (EMPG) (customer-funded)
- **Funds:** general state and local emergency-management capability. A cost match is required.
- **Size:** FY2026 **$337.25M**, 56 awards.
- **Who applies:** states. Local emergency-management offices receive funds as subrecipients.
- **Status:** **Recurring.** The FY2026 application closed **Jul 15, 2026** (DHS-26-GPD-042-01-99). The next window is expected around mid-2027.
- **DIZA fit:** a county emergency-management office could pay for a resident check-in and alert-call line out of EMPG plus its local match.
- **URL:** https://www.fema.gov/grants/preparedness/emergency-management-performance · https://simpler.grants.gov/opportunity/362796

### 5. ASPR Hospital Preparedness Program (HPP) (customer-funded, secondary)
- **Funds:** healthcare coalition readiness.
- **Size:** Budget Period 3 (Jul 1, 2026-Jun 30, 2027) has **$240M** for 62 recipients, which are states and territories.
- **Who applies:** state health departments, which then pass funds to healthcare coalitions.
- **Status:** **Active.** This is the continuing FY2024-2028 cooperative agreement.
- **DIZA fit:** coalitions could use DIZA for hospital and patient call surges and for checking on medically vulnerable residents.
- **URL:** https://aspr.hhs.gov/newsroom/Pages/FY2026-BP3-HPP-Continuation-Guidance.aspx

## Recommendation
- **PRIMARY: NSF SBIR Phase I (NSF 26-510).** It accepts any topic, pays $305K with no equity, recurs three times a year and runs Phase II up to $1.25M. The **Mar 4, 2027** deadline is realistic.
- **ALTERNATIVE: a customer-funded city or county pilot.** The LA-area emergency-management office buys DIZA with HSGP/UASI or EMPG funds. This is slower because it follows the grant cycle, but it proves real demand. Run it alongside SBIR, not instead of it.

## How we'd apply (3 steps)
1. **Now to Q1 2027:** take USC's Regional I-Corps course, then apply for National I-Corps Teams (up to $50K). Interview 100+ people at LA 311, EOCs, 911 centers and campus emergency management. Form an LLC or C-corp and register it with SAM.gov.
2. **Q4 2026 to Mar 2027:** submit an NSF Project Pitch built on the I-Corps findings. Once invited, submit the Phase I proposal by **Mar 4, 2027**. Frame the innovation as surge-triage AI plus detecting vulnerable callers, not the voice platform. Get a support letter from a city or campus partner.
3. **2027:** run a pilot with an LA city or county emergency-management office or USC. Help them write DIZA into their next **UASI/EMPG** project list (the FY2027 cycle opens around Jun-Jul) so the pilot becomes a paid contract. Use Phase I results to support Phase II.
