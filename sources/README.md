## Kickoff deck: points not in the hub
- **Comms vertical one-liner:** "In a crisis, call lines flood faster than people can answer. How do we get the right callers to a human first?"
- **Three tests for your idea:**
  - **Ordinary day:** who pays on a calm Tuesday, and why?
  - **Crisis:** what does it do at 3 AM when the network is down and the staff are gone?
  - **Travel** (if time): does it work with a tenth of the budget? Check place, network, language, price, trust.
  - "Say which way you lean, out loud, before a judge says it for you."
- **The twists:**
  - Each twist changes the problem for **every** track.
  - Exact times and the twist text land **in the app and on WhatsApp**.
  - Working them in is encouraged, not required.
- **Sunday:**
  - Devpost links lock at **11 AM**.
  - Confirm your demo in the app by **9 AM**, or you get no judge feedback.
- **IBM:** IBM + NASA open geospatial models on Hugging Face (flood mapping, burn scars, weather).
- **Mentors:**
  - 2 constant mentors per team, matched to the "where you want help" ticks.
  - Pairings are published overnight.
  - Bring one decision question, not a status update.
